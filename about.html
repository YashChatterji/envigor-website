About Envigor Beverages

The category that didn’t exist yet.

Three billion Cola servings are consumed across the world every day. The formula hasn’t meaningfully changed in over a hundred years, and neither has the choice it forces: enjoy what you love, or protect how you live.

Envigor Beverages was built to end that choice.

We didn’t set out to make a healthier Cola. We set out to make a better one, built to a standard the category never bothered to hold itself to, and confident enough to stand next to anything already on the shelf.

Voila is the first result of that work. Not a wellness drink wearing a Cola label, but Cola itself, rebuilt from a blank page by people who spent decades formulating for the biggest names in the industry, and who thought the biggest names had stopped pushing forward.

This is bigger than one can. It’s the start of a new category, one where taste was never the trade-off, and where the people who drink Cola every day finally have something made for how they actually want to live.

Voila is looking for retailers, distributors, and partners who want in early.
